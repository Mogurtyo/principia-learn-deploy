Principia.learn BETA — Data Protection Notice

This notice explains what the Principia.learn beta app stores about you. It applies to the beta build distributed for the Modelling Matter module.

What we store, and where

You are…Stored whereWhat is stored
Using the app as a guest Only on your own device (browser storage / app data) Your name, lesson progress, scores, mistakes, and daily usage minutes
Signed in with an account Your device and your account in our database (Supabase, EU/US cloud) Everything above, synced to your account so progress follows you between devices

Why we store it

  • Progress and scores — so you can pick up where you left off, and so the module team can see whether the app helps learning.
  • Daily usage minutes and session counts — the module team uses these to measure how practice time relates to improvement. They are stored as totals per day; they do not track you in real time and are never linked to anything beyond your account.

Cookies / storage choice

The app uses device storage (and account storage when you sign in) to keep your progress. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. When you first sign in you can refuse storage — if you refuse, your progress will not be saved between visits. You can change this at any time in Settings → Data & cookies.

Who can see it

  • Nobody outside the module team. Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Your individual answers and scores are visible only to you. The module team sees progress and usage statistics for the purpose of evaluating this beta.

How long we keep it

This is a beta test app only. When the term ends, all identifiable information — names, accounts, and anything linked to them — is deleted. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (e.g. “students who practised ~10 minutes a day improved their scores by X%”) may be kept to report on the beta.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete everything stored about you at any time: use Settings → Export progress for a copy, Settings → Reset progress to delete your device copy, or contact the module team to delete your account and its data.

Questions

Contact the module team via your course page or VLE.
