Principia.learn BETA — Data Protection Notice

This notice explains what the Principia.learn beta app stores about you. It applies to the beta build distributed for the Modelling Matter module.

The per-question record

Since version 3.9.0 the app keeps a short log of how you work through questions, not only how you scored on them. For each question you meet, it records:

  • What happened — whether you answered it correctly, answered it wrongly, or skipped it. A skip is recorded as a skip, not as a wrong answer.
  • How long it took you, in whole seconds. Anything over ten minutes is recorded as ten minutes, so a question left open while you make a cup of tea does not read as ten minutes of thinking.
  • Which answer you chose — for a multiple-choice or fill-the-blank question, which of the offered options you picked; for a drag-into-order question, the order you put the steps in, and only when it was wrong.
  • How many mis-taps you made on a matching exercise before completing it.
  • Which question it was, as a short fingerprint of the question text. The wording of the question itself is not stored.

For each lesson it also records whether you finished it or left part way through, how far you got, and how long you spent. Previously an abandoned lesson left no trace at all, which made a student who started five lessons and gave up on four of them look identical to one who never opened the app.

This log is capped at the 300 most recent entries and rolls: once it is full, the oldest entry is dropped each time a new one is added. It is not a full-term history, and it is roughly two to three weeks of steady use.

Two limits are deliberate, and the app is built so that they hold:

  • Nothing you type is stored as text. The only question type you type into is the numeric one, and only the number the app reads out of what you typed is kept. The characters you actually typed are never stored, never uploaded and never seen by anyone.
  • No record carries a time of day. Every entry holds a duration and a calendar date, and nothing finer. The app cannot tell — and the module team cannot ask it — whether you were revising at three in the morning, or studying during somebody else's lecture.

Why we store it

  • Progress and scores — so you can pick up where you left off, and so the module team can see whether the app helps learning.
  • Daily usage minutes and session counts — the module team uses these to measure how practice time relates to improvement. They are stored as totals per day; they do not track you in real time and are never linked to anything beyond your account.
  • The per-question record — so the module team can tell a badly worded question from a genuinely hard one, see which wrong answer tempts people, and find the point in a lesson where students stop. These are questions about the course material, answered across the whole cohort; they are not a report card on any individual.

Cookies / storage choice

The app uses device storage (and account storage when you sign in) to keep your progress. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. After you sign in, Principia asks in a dialog whether it may save your progress, and asks again each time you sign in afterwards. You can refuse — if you do, your progress will not be saved between visits, and because that is easy to tap by accident and impossible to notice until weeks later, refusing asks you to confirm once before it takes effect. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Data & cookies.

Who can see it

  • Nobody outside the module team. Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Your individual answers and scores are visible only to you. The module team sees progress and usage statistics for the purpose of evaluating this beta.

How long we keep it

This is a beta test app only. When the term ends, all identifiable information — names, accounts, and anything linked to them — is deleted. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (e.g. “students who practised ~10 minutes a day improved their scores by X%”) may be kept to report on the beta.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete everything stored about you at any time: use Settings → Export progress for a copy, Settings → Reset all progress to delete your device copy, or contact the module team to delete your account and its data.
